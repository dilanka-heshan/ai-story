# Final Quality Gates

## Character/visual
- [ ] Faces, hair, skin, height, clothes match master references
- [ ] No extra fingers/limbs, morphing faces or inconsistent props
- [ ] Scene geography and lighting match adjacent shots
- [ ] Consistent stylized 3D look; not switching into photorealism/anime

## Story/edit
- [ ] First 3 seconds offer a clear hook
- [ ] Each shot advances the story; no dead time
- [ ] Story works with sound off (captions + visual story)
- [ ] Dialogue and music do not overlap unpleasantly
- [ ] Subtitles remain inside safe mobile viewing area

## Publishing/compliance
- [ ] Music, stock media, voices and generated content cleared for commercial use
- [ ] Synthetic-content labels/disclosures checked when relevant
- [ ] Export verified on phone; platform upload not clipped
- [ ] Description and thumbnail match the actual video

## Process
- [ ] Every prompt version committed
- [ ] Every generated asset logged with exact input references and settings
- [ ] Rejected attempts and why recorded
- [ ] Total time, credits, and lessons learned documented
